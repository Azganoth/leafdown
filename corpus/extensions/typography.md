# Typography Extensions

## Highlighting

==highlighted text== and ==highlight with **strong content**==.

## Subscript and superscript

H~2~O and 2^10^ use tilde and caret extensions.

Escaped H\~2\~O and 2\^10\^ remain boundary cases.

## Insertion and deletion

++inserted text++ is an optional insertion candidate.

{--old--}{++new++} is a CriticMarkup-style deletion and addition candidate.
