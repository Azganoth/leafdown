## Equivalent-looking Unicode

The words “café” and “café” use precomposed and combining accents respectively.
They should remain distinct source sequences even when they look alike.

Curly punctuation—“quotes”, ‘apostrophes’, and an em dash—appears beside plain
ASCII punctuation. Emoji remain ordinary text: 🌱 💧 ✅.
